1. Who We Are

Nestly Parenting ("we," "us," or "our") is a parenting content platform operated by Attaullah. Our website is located at nestlyparenting.com. We publish educational articles about pregnancy, newborn care, and early parenthood, primarily serving readers in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at: attaullah@nestlyparenting.com

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact form submissions: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content.
  • Comments (Giscus): If you comment on our articles using Giscus (GitHub-authenticated), your GitHub profile information is processed by Giscus. We do not store this data directly. Please review Giscus's privacy policy.
  • Comments (Facebook): If you choose to comment using the Facebook Comments option instead, your Facebook profile information (such as your name and profile picture) is processed by Meta/Facebook, not by us. Please review Facebook's Data Policy.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: We may use Google Analytics or similar tools to collect anonymised information about how visitors use our site, including pages viewed, time spent, device type, and approximate geographic location.
  • Log data: Our web hosting provider may automatically collect server log data including IP addresses, browser type, and referring URLs.
  • Local Storage: Our Feeding Tracker tool stores data (feeding session logs) locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your contact form enquiries
  • To understand how our content is being used and improve it
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense (see Section 5)
  • To comply with our legal obligations
  • To protect the security and integrity of our website

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand visitor behaviour (e.g., Google Analytics). These are anonymised and can be opted out of.
  • Advertising cookies: Set by Google AdSense to serve personalised advertisements based on your browsing behaviour.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Opting out of advertising cookies will result in less relevant (but still present) advertisements.

UK/EU visitors: Under UK GDPR and the EU Cookie Law, non-essential cookies require your consent. By continuing to use our site, you acknowledge our use of cookies as described above.

5. Google AdSense & Advertising

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our site, please visit: Google's Privacy & Terms.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — Website analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — Advertising. Privacy Policy
  • Giscus — Comment system (GitHub-authenticated). Privacy Policy
  • Facebook Comments (Meta) — Alternative comment system (Facebook-authenticated). Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash — Photography (used for article images). Privacy Policy
  • Bootstrap CDN (jsDelivr) — CSS framework delivery. Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to object: Object to our processing of your data for certain purposes.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Right to opt out of sale (US — CCPA): We do not sell personal data. California residents have rights under the CCPA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at attaullah@nestlyparenting.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for up to 12 months, after which they are securely deleted. Analytics data is retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default), after which it is anonymised. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

Nestly Parenting is an educational resource for adults — specifically parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (US), 16 (EU/UK), or 13 (Canada). If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
