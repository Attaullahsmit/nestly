The Story Behind Nestly

Why We Started

When you search for parenting advice online, you're met with two extremes: overly clinical medical jargon that feels cold and distant, or social-media-polished content that makes parenthood look effortless and perfect. Neither actually helps a parent who is scared, exhausted, and just trying to do their best.

Nestly Parenting was built to fill the gap in between. We publish 4–5 articles a week covering everything from the first positive pregnancy test to the sleepless nights of the newborn period — written with warmth, grounded in evidence, and always honest about the parts that are hard.

Our audience is primarily first-time parents in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom — three countries with very different healthcare systems, very different parental leave policies, and very similar fears when that positive test appears. We write for all of them.

Our Mission

To make new parenthood feel less lonely. To give every first-time parent — regardless of income, background, or support network — access to the kind of honest, practical, and emotionally intelligent guidance that used to only exist if you were lucky enough to have the right people around you.

⚕️ A note on our content: Nestly Parenting is an educational resource, not a medical provider. Our articles are thoroughly researched and draw from reputable sources including the NHS (UK), American Academy of Pediatrics (US), and Health Canada. However, they are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult your GP, OB-GYN, midwife, or paediatrician with specific health concerns.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🤝
Honesty First

We don't sugarcoat parenthood. If something is hard, we say it's hard. Our readers trust us because we tell the truth.

🔬
Evidence-Based

Every article is researched using reputable sources — NHS, AAP, Health Canada, peer-reviewed studies. We cite our sources.

💛
Non-Judgmental

Breastfeed or formula. Natural birth or c-section. Stay home or return to work. We support parents, not agendas.

🌍
Inclusive

We write for mothers, fathers, partners, single parents, and every family structure. Parenthood belongs to everyone.
